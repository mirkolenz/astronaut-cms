A year ago Meridian was three people and a shared document full of hopes.
Today it is three people, nine shipped projects, and a much shorter document.
Here is some of what the year taught us.

## Say no earlier

The projects that went well were the ones we scoped honestly at the start.
The ones that hurt were the ones we said yes to before we understood them.

Turning down good-but-wrong work is how you protect the work that is right.

## Small is a feature

We were nervous that clients would want a bigger team.
It turned out the opposite was true.

People liked knowing exactly who was doing the work, and that the person who designed a screen was the person who built it.

## Ship, then improve

Our best decisions were the ones we made in public, in front of real users.
Our worst were the ones we polished in private for too long.

Getting something real in front of people, early, beat every internal debate we tried to win first.

## Onward

Year two is about doing less, more deliberately.
Fewer projects, longer partnerships, and the same small team that got us here.
